Assumptions

  • Inspection $15k, exposure $300k, 90% catch rate

  • Low NIC values mostly mean interference
  • B3 satellites ~$3M each

Constraints

  • Sentinel-2 every 5 days; monsoon clouds
  • One day of ADS-B data so far
  • Satellites cannot confirm runway certification, ATC or slots
  • Rebooking needs airline systems
