Noida International Airport (Jewar): construction progress from orbit

Method: a 10 m pixel counts as cleared when its highest NDVI over the trailing 12 months (July–September monsoon scenes excluded) stays below 0.3. That removes the crop cycle, so farmland never counts as cleared. Cloud and shadow are masked with the scene classification layer. Dates with under 60% clear footprint are gaps. Grass, scrub and ponds left inside the airport stay "not cleared", so 100% is not expected. This measures land conversion, not completion of buildings.
